# ADR-0030: Business Profile, one GST rule set, and the Settings layout

- Status: Accepted and implemented as the first post-FUB usability task (2026-10-23). R-040 stays open; this does not enable production billing.
- Builds on: ADR-0015 (first-run setup), ADR-0023 (tax configuration, review fingerprint and the production gate), ADR-0024 (immutable invoice snapshots)

## Problem

After setup there was no screen to correct the seller's GST registration, so Production Readiness could say "Seller GST registration recorded and valid: NOT READY" with no way to fix it. Inspecting the code also showed two defects behind it: the readiness check judged a GSTIN with its own shape-only pattern while setup also checked the check character (two GSTIN rules), and a long Settings area with blank space and extra scrolling.

## Decisions

1. **Authoritative model (two levels, always written together).** Legal identity and registration status live on the **organization**: legal name, trade name, business type, PAN, GST status, GSTIN, state. The address and the registration of the **primary business location** live on that location: name, address lines, city, state, state code, PIN, GSTIN. Setup writes the same GSTIN and state to both. Invoicing takes the location's GSTIN and state first and the organization's second; the production gate reads the organization's. Therefore a Business Profile save updates the organization and its primary location **in one transaction**; if any step fails neither changes. Other (future) locations keep their own GST registrations and are never touched, so multiple registrations remain possible later.
2. **One GST rule set.** `sellerRegistrationProblems` (in `@commercebill/setup`) is the only place that decides whether PAN, GSTIN, GST status and state are acceptable: PAN shape; GSTIN required for REGISTERED and forbidden for UNREGISTERED; shape and check character; the GSTIN's state digits against the state; the PAN inside the GSTIN. First-run setup, the Business Profile (server and browser) and the readiness check (`sellerGateProblems`) all use it. The tax engine's own copy was removed. The rules check format and consistency only; nothing contacts the GST portal and a valid format does not prove a GSTIN exists or belongs to the business.
3. **Consequence for the gate.** A GSTIN that only matches the old shape pattern but fails the check character is now judged "not valid" by Production Readiness. Setup could never have stored such a value; only test fixtures did, and the fixtures now use a valid fictional GSTIN.
4. **Review fingerprint unchanged.** The tax-configuration fingerprint covers rule-set versions, jurisdictions, categories and rates, not seller identity. The seller requirement is evaluated independently on every readiness read. A Business Profile change therefore never makes a tax review stale and a seller correction is reflected in Production Readiness immediately (tested). No new invalidation rule was added.
5. **API.** `GET /business-profile` (`org.settings.read`), `PUT /business-profile` (`org.settings.update`). Both permissions already existed; no permission was added and nothing checks a role name. Unauthenticated 401; authenticated without the key 403; Viewer/Auditor and Accountant read only (`canEdit: false`). The organization always comes from the session; the body is a strict schema (an unknown key such as `organizationId` is refused). Saves carry `expectedVersion`; a stale form gets 409 and nothing is overwritten.
6. **Audit.** Each material save writes `organization.profile.update`: actor, organization, timestamp, correlation id and the **names** of the changed fields. PAN, GST status, GSTIN and state also keep before and after values (tax-significant). Other values (names, address) are not copied into the audit trail. Diagnostic logs never carry the body or the seller details (tested with trace logging). A save that changes nothing writes nothing.
7. **Posted invoices are untouched.** Invoices carry an immutable seller snapshot taken at posting; the service never reads or writes it. A regression test posts an invoice, edits the profile, and proves the snapshot columns are identical while the next invoice uses the new facts.
8. **Not stored, therefore not shown.** Phone, email, website, bank details and logo have no persistence in the domain model; the screen lists them as "not stored yet" instead of accepting values it would lose (DEF-11).
9. **Fix actions.** Production Readiness (Dashboard and Tax) shows **Fix** only for unmet items the application can correct and only to people who may open the target: seller registration to Business Profile, tax review to Tax Configuration, HSN/SAC policy to Tax Configuration, backup to Backup & Recovery.
10. **Settings layout.** The Settings landing is a compact grouped index (Business, Tax, Data protection, System) of areas that exist; there are no placeholder pages. Two layout causes were fixed: (a) the shell's `<main>` was not positioned, so absolutely positioned screen-reader text sat outside the window and stretched the whole document (Dashboard and Tax pages measured 981 and 861 px in a 700 px window: a blank strip and a second scrollbar); `<main>` is now `relative` and the shell clips, so only the content area scrolls; (b) card grids switched to two columns at a _window_ width of 1280 px although the page beside the sidebar is about 220 px narrower, so 1000 px windows got one long column and cards stretched to the tallest card in their row; `CardGrid` now follows the width of its own area and keeps each card's natural height.

## Consequences

- The business has one obvious place to maintain seller facts, and Production Readiness shows how to reach it.
- Editing the GSTIN by hand to something format-valid but wrong is still possible; only a person can know (R-040, R-118).
- Phone/email/website/bank/logo remain absent (DEF-11) until a later task adds storage and the documents that use them.
